Privacy Policy & Imprint

For the iPhone app Future. Last updated: 11 August 2026

In short

Future keeps everything on your iPhone. There is no user account, no sign-in and no server that holds your assets, debts, contracts or documents. There is no advertising, no tracking and no analytics library of any kind.

What the app stores

Everything you enter — assets, quantities, debts, savings goals, fixed costs, contracts and notes — is stored solely on your device using Apple SwiftData. It is never transmitted to the controllers, because there is no server it could be transmitted to.

What leaves your device

To display prices, news and descriptions, the app queries public sources. These requests contain no information about you — only the ticker symbol currently being displayed. No account is used and no identifier is set.

The legal basis for these requests is the legitimate interest in providing the app's core functionality (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Documents you import

Loan agreements, portfolio statements and contracts are analysed entirely on your device using Apple PDFKit and Vision. Neither the file nor the text recognised from it ever leaves your iPhone. Photos you take for this purpose are used only for text recognition.

AI assistant

The assistant is optional and only becomes usable once you enter your own API key in Settings. Your question is then transmitted to Anthropic PBC (anthropic.com) and answered there.

  • Your own figures are included only if you switch on “Answer using my figures”. That switch is off by default.
  • While it is off, the request contains nothing but the text of your question.
  • The API key is held in your device keychain, readable only while the device is unlocked, and is not synced to iCloud.

The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by enabling the feature and withdrawable at any time by switching it off. Processing may take place in the United States.

Widget

Net worth, currency and history are shared with the widget through a protected container that only this app and its widget can read. Please note that the widget can display your net worth on the lock screen.

Notifications

Reminders for fixed costs, cancellation deadlines and price alerts are scheduled locally on your device. There are no push messages from any server.

Purchases

Should paid features be offered, Apple handles the transaction. The controllers receive no payment data.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Austria the Datenschutzbehörde.

In practice: because all data resides on your device, it is entirely under your control. Settings → Export produces a copy as a file. Deleting the app removes everything; no copy exists anywhere else.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If anything about the processing changes, this page will be updated. The date at the top reflects the current version.
